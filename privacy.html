3. Provision of the website (hosting on GitHub Pages)

Provider. The website is provided via GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub delivers the pages through the content delivery network of Fastly, Inc., USA (GitHub lists Fastly as a “Content delivery service” in its list of subprocessors).

Data processed. With every request, GitHub processes the connection data required for delivery, in particular the IP address of your device, the date and time of the request, the requested file and the details transmitted by your browser (e.g. browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL). According to GitHub, the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes (GitHub Docs, “Data collection”). IP addresses are treated as personal data (Art. 4(1) GDPR; cf. Court of Justice of the EU, judgment of 19 October 2016 – C‑582/14, Breyer).

Purpose and legal basis (Art. 13(1)(c) and (d) GDPR). The processing serves to deliver the website, to ensure its stability and security, and to detect and prevent attacks and abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in the secure and functional provision of this website (cf. Recital 49 GDPR; Court of Justice of the EU, C‑582/14, Breyer).

Retention (Art. 13(2)(a) GDPR). GitHub does not specify a fixed period for GitHub Pages logs (as of November 2026). According to the GitHub General Privacy Statement, the retention period depends on the purpose of collection and on legal obligations; data are kept as needed to fulfil contractual obligations, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. I have no access to these logs, receive no visitor statistics and cannot identify individual visitors.

Recipients (Art. 13(1)(e) GDPR). GitHub, Inc. and Fastly, Inc. as a service provider engaged by GitHub. Insofar as GitHub processes the connection data for its own purposes, in particular the security of its services, GitHub acts as a controller in its own right according to its privacy information (GitHub, Inc. or GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, the Netherlands); to that extent the GitHub General Privacy Statement applies.

Transfer to the USA (Art. 13(1)(f) GDPR). The data are also processed in the USA. The transfer to GitHub, Inc. and Fastly, Inc. is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45(1) GDPR in conjunction with Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023). According to their own statements (GitHub, Fastly), both companies are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the certifications can be viewed in the U.S. Department of Commerce’s Data Privacy Framework List. For transfers to third countries without an adequacy decision, GitHub states that it relies on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021), which are available on EUR-Lex (see section 15). The General Court of the European Union dismissed the action for annulment of the adequacy decision by judgment of 3 September 2025 (Case T‑553/23, Latombe v Commission); an appeal is pending before the Court of Justice (Case C‑703/25 P). This policy will be updated should the legal situation change.

4. Cookies, tracking and access to terminal equipment

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics, audience-measurement, tracking or advertising services and carries out no profiling. Typefaces, scripts and graphics are delivered exclusively by this website itself (GitHub Pages, section 3) and not by third-party providers; no third-party content – such as web-font services, external script libraries, social-media plugins, embedded videos or maps – is embedded.

The website stores no information on your terminal equipment; in particular, neither cookies nor local storage or session storage are used. The website’s scripts merely evaluate display characteristics locally in your browser (such as window size, pixel density and the “reduce motion” setting) without transmitting this information. Insofar as this, or the caching of retrieved files by your browser (browser cache), constituted access to or storage of information within the meaning of § 25(1) of the Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG), it would be permitted under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, because it is strictly necessary to provide the digital service you have expressly requested. Consent is therefore not required, and no consent banner is used.

6. Contact by email

Data processed and purpose. If you email me, I process your email address, your name (if given), the content of your message including any attachments, and the related metadata (e.g. time of sending) in order to handle and answer your enquiry.

Legal basis. Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as you yourself are a party to a contract with me that is envisaged or already exists. If you write on behalf of an organisation (such as a university or a company, e.g. regarding a position) or in any other case, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in properly answering the enquiries addressed to me.

Processor. The mailbox is operated by checkdomain GmbH, Große Burgstraße 27/29, 23552 Lübeck, Germany, as processor (Art. 4(8) GDPR) on servers in Germany. No transfer to third countries takes place in this respect.

Retention. The data are erased once they are no longer necessary for handling the enquiry and any resulting collaboration (Art. 17(1)(a) GDPR); correspondence without an ongoing collaboration is erased at the latest two years after the last message. This does not apply where further storage is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Art. 17(3)(e) GDPR).

Confidentiality. Emails are normally transport-encrypted (TLS) between the servers involved but are not end-to-end encrypted. Please do not send special categories of personal data (Art. 9(1) GDPR) by email.

10. Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions, you have the following rights (Art. 13(2)(b) GDPR):

  • access to the data concerning you, including a copy (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • notification of any rectification, erasure or restriction to recipients and, on request, information about those recipients (Art. 19 GDPR),
  • data portability, where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and carried out by automated means (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR, see section 11).

Requests may be made informally by email to yaman@yevkaya.com. They will be answered without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt; this period may be extended by two further months under the conditions of Art. 12(3), second sentence, GDPR. Handling is in principle free of charge (Art. 12(5) GDPR). Where there are reasonable doubts as to the identity of the requesting person, additional information necessary to confirm their identity may be requested (Art. 12(6) GDPR).

As no processing is based on consent, there is no right of withdrawal within the meaning of Art. 7(3) GDPR (Art. 13(2)(c) GDPR).

11. Right to object under Art. 21 GDPR

You have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of personal data concerning you which is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21(1) GDPR).

The data will then no longer be processed unless compelling legitimate grounds for the processing can be demonstrated which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. The objection may be sent informally to yaman@yevkaya.com. As I have no access to GitHub’s server logs, an objection to that processing may additionally be addressed directly to GitHub.

12. Right to lodge a complaint and judicial remedy

Without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77(1) GDPR). The authority competent for the controller is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de (Art. 51 and Art. 55(1) GDPR; § 40(1) BDSG in conjunction with Art. 18(1), first sentence, of the Bavarian Data Protection Act, BayDSG). You also have the right to an effective judicial remedy (Art. 79 GDPR).
